# Kebabpunk / Legend of Kebablance

## Overview
Back when you were an adventurer, you cannot forget the ultimate food you have tasted in the distant lands called Adana. It was called Doner Kebab as you remember.

Moving your way from dungeons to dungeons now it is time for a retirement from your adventurer career. It is time to open your Doner kebab buffet to serve this folk and other adventurers passing by.

If you thought your adventurer life is over, well... Adventure never ends. 

Serve doner kebabs to the folk as their orders. Involve into other adventurers' story. Help people for good. 

## Installation
Clone into a folder and import the project into Unity Hub with editor version 6000.0.64f1
